The Seeed Odyssey X86 J4105 supports M.2 NVMe SSDs in both 2280 and 2242 sizes, and a fast NVMe drive delivers a significant performance boost for this board. For a focus on **reliability** and solid performance, the Micron Crucial P510 and Samsung 990 EVO Plus both have excellent reputations, 5-year warranties, and positive feedback for stability and endurance.[1][2][3]

## Recommended NVMe SSDs

### Micron Crucial P510
- **PCIe Gen 5×4** interface, but also works in Gen 3/4 slots, so compatibility isn't an issue
- Known for excellent performance, runs relatively cool, and comes with a **5-year warranty**
- Affordable price point relative to performance and reliability
- Capacity up to 2TB, but smaller sizes (500GB–1TB) are usually considered most reliable due to higher endurance per cell.

### Samsung 990 EVO Plus
- **PCIe Gen 5×2** (works in older slots at lower speeds)
- Top-tier reliability, cool running, also with a **5-year warranty**
- Samsung Magician software for drive management and diagnostics
- Frequently recommended for mission-critical usage.

### Additional Reliable Options
- **SK hynix Gold P31**: Known for stability and power efficiency in the PCIe Gen 3 segment.
- **Crucial P5 Plus**: Well balanced for reliability and cost, with several years of high user and reviewer marks.

## Price and Sizing Guidance

- For the best balance of **reliability** and reasonable price, choose a 500GB to 1TB drive unless a larger size is absolutely needed. Smaller drives typically have even higher endurance and generate less heat.
- Aim for a drive with a 5-year warranty and a good user reputation for reliability, rather than the absolute fastest (which may cost more and add little practical benefit for this platform).

## Compatibility Notes

- The Odyssey X86 J4105 accepts M.2 NVMe SSDs in 2242 and 2280 sizes; either form factor will work.[1]
- Any of the above recommendations are compatible—just verify the physical length (2280 is the most common and widely available).[1]
